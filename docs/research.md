# Research notes

Checked 2026-11-04.

- Intelex publishes one price: Safety Essentials "starting at $44 USD/User/Month (Minimum 25 Users, Billed Annually)". Essentials includes incident management, action plans and inspections, with training, document control, meetings and observations as add-ons. Management of Change is not listed in Essentials; the Advanced and Enterprise plans are quoted privately. Source: https://www.intelex.com/request-price
- Intelex's Management of Change page describes change requests through "evaluation, design and analysis, implementation, and review phases", checklists and approvals, and OSHA 1910.119 compliance. It does not mention pre-start-up safety reviews or temporary change expiry. Source: https://www.intelex.com/products/applications/management-of-change-software/
- The rules in docs/compliance.md were read on the official sites (legislation.govt.nz, safeworkaustralia.gov.au, ecfr.gov) on the same date.
