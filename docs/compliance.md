# Compliance checks and their sources

Checked 2026-11-04. `/compliance` runs these checks against the records. They support the site's own review; they are not a compliance certificate, legal advice, or a substitute for applying the law to your plant. Each rule below says what the law or standard says, what this build checks, and where it comes from.

The checks borrow their shape from the US process safety standard (OSHA 29 CFR 1910.119), because it spells out what a management of change procedure must consider. New Zealand and Australian law ask for the same discipline in broader words. Where a timing in this build is a local policy rather than the law, it says so.

## The duty underneath

In New Zealand a PCBU must ensure, so far as is reasonably practicable, "the provision and maintenance of safe systems of work" and "the safe use, handling, and storage of plant, substances, and structures" ([Health and Safety at Work Act 2015, s 36(3)](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2015/70/en/latest/#DLM5976866)). Australian model WHS Regulations reg 38(3) counts "a change to a system of work, a process or a procedure" as a change at the workplace that triggers a review of controls. A change control record is how a site shows it did that.

## What this build does not decide

- Whether a change is a "replacement in kind" (1910.119(b): "a replacement which satisfies the design specification"). That is an engineering call; record it on the change and cancel it with the reason if it needs no further control.
- Whether your site is a major hazard facility, or lower or upper tier. Set `major_hazard` on the site from your notification to the regulator.
- State law. The model WHS Regulations take effect through each state's own regulations, and numbering can differ. Victoria has its own Occupational Health and Safety Regulations 2017. Confirm the regulation numbers for your state before you rely on them.
- Sign-off roles and the 7-day emergency window are local policy, set in `approval_rules` and this file. Change them with `/customise`.
